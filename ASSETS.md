## Book artwork prompt

A beautifully crafted miniature paper sculpture of an open cream-colored book, seen from a slightly elevated three-quarter angle, its pages unfolding into a playful archway of layered forest-green paper leaves, terracotta semicircles, a warm mustard-yellow sphere and slender pencil-like wooden shapes. High-end tactile cut-paper and matte ceramic material, soft natural sunlight from upper left, realistic soft shadows, warm off-white studio background #F7F4ED. Elegant art-directed still life, not cartoon, not shiny plastic, not futuristic. Generous breathing room, central composition, square format. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no mathematical answers. This is decorative cover artwork, not a teaching diagram. The book remains clearly recognizable and the main subject.
